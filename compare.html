La Festa, or Sailing Club

My read

Keep La Festa. The last three days are not three days near Sunset Town, they are three days inside it. Kiss Bridge, the free show, the Positano walk and the clock tower cost you nothing because you sleep in the middle of them. From Sailing Club all four collapse into one long Saturday, and if that evening rains you have used your only shot.

Sailing Club wins on the two things that are genuinely annoying about La Festa: halal dinner and the early flight. It also has private pool villas and a kids club, which La Festa does not. If the last days were meant to be a resort collapse rather than an itinerary, it would be the better hotel.

Two things I would not overlook. It is 800 metres back from the beach with an hourly shuttle, which guests specifically complain about, and with a six month old that is daily friction. And Long Beach is the west coast, which is the exposed side in the southwest monsoon, while the southern tip is the sheltered one. Those are the same August winds already flagged in your plan.
